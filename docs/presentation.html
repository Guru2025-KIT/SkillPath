04 · Algorithms & Adaptive Pathing

How gaps become roadmaps

📐 Gap Score Formula
gap_score = level_delta × 2.5 × importance_weight

level_delta = required_idx − current_idx
(none=0, beginner=1, intermediate=2, advanced=3, expert=4)

importance_weight: critical=1.0, important=0.75, nice-to-have=0.45

Result clamped to [0, 10]. Strengths (delta ≤ 0) are excluded from pathway entirely.
🔍 Proficiency Inference
Two signals checked per skill mention:

1. Years regex — "3 years of Python" → intermediate
2. Context window ±80 chars — "senior" / "expert" / "familiar with" mapped to level tiers.

Longest alias matched first to prevent partial matches.
🗺️ Adaptive Pathing (NetworkX)
Step 1 — Build DiGraph:
Edge (docker → kubernetes) = "learn docker before k8s".
Transitive prereqs included automatically.

Step 2 — Topological sort:
nx.topological_sort(G) → valid learning order.

Step 3 — Phase binning:
Phase 1: critical gaps, score ≥ 6
Phase 2: critical/important, score ≥ 3
Phase 3: important, score < 6
Phase 4: nice-to-have (catch-all)

Step 4 — Course match:
Best catalog entry by from_level + target_level proximity.

Duration estimate: total hours ÷ 10 hrs/week.
